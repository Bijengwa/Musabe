# Musabe Schools - curriculum and syllabus files (TIE)

Downloaded 19 Nov 2026 from www.tie.go.tz (official). 49 PDFs.

## 00-Pre-primary (2 files)
- Curriculum and Syllabus for Pre-primary Education 2023 - English and Kiswahili.
- Government pre-primary is ONE year (child starts at age 5). 194 days, 2 terms, 3.5 hours a day, 20-minute periods.
- There is NO government syllabus for Baby Class or Middle Class. The school sets its own.

## 01-Primary (18 files) - new curriculum, Standard I-VI
- Curriculum book (English + Kiswahili).
- Std I-II: one combined syllabus (English + Kiswahili).
- Std III-VI: Mathematics/Hisabati, Science/Sayansi, Geography and Environment/Jiografia na Mazingira,
  Arts and Sports/Sanaa na Michezo (each English + Kiswahili); English; Kiswahili;
  Historia ya Tanzania na Maadili; French; Arabic; Chinese.
- Standard VII is NOT in the new curriculum (it is being phased out). Class VII still uses the old 2015/2019 syllabi, which are not in this folder.

## 02-Secondary (29 files) - Form I-IV, 2023
- Curriculum book (corrected 2026; TIE's English and Kiswahili links are the same file).
- Syllabi: Mathematics, Additional Mathematics (F3-4), English Language, Literature in English (F3-4),
  Kiswahili (2023 and the newer 2025 version), Fasihi ya Kiswahili (F3-4), Physics, Chemistry, Biology,
  Geography, History, Historia ya Tanzania na Maadili, Computer Science, Business Studies, Book-keeping,
  Agriculture, Food and Human Nutrition, Textile and Garment Construction, Fine Art, Music, Theatre Arts,
  Sport Studies, Bible Knowledge, Elimu ya Dini ya Kiislamu, French, Arabic (Kiarabu), Chinese.
- Elimu ya Dini ya Kiislamu is a scanned PDF (no text layer); it needs OCR before data can be pulled out.
